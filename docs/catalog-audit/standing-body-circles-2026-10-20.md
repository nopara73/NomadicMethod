# Standing Body Circles source and admission review

The movement was approved for catalog preparation after movement and media
review, before assigning anatomy. The final packaged workout review, source
audits and required Android/web catalog gates subsequently passed.

The source is Mbodies Training Academy / Pilates Consultant's
[Postural Sway](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T8Ew_ghNgy0). The publisher
describes planted forward/back, lateral and circular sway in both directions.
[Eldergym's Body Circles](https://eldergym.com/exercises-for-improving-balance/)
independently describes the same straight-body drill as deliberate ankle work.
This is an established ankle-control exercise, not an invented combination.

`6a2d2dfac74bcafc28d6693dcd69253e0b230647d7f945ef8a5a5bd7bec88a70`.
The selected interval is 66.28–70.04 seconds. Its elevated view shows a complete
clockwise circle, both planted feet and the whole person, with no camera change,
caption, hold or travel. The end was shortened from the first review's 70.20
boundary to reduce the loop's pose reset. The native 94-frame interval is retained
in `tools/ReviewedSourceMedia/body-circles-mbodies-66.28-3.76.mp4`, SHA-256
`4189da4666ff523eade19bf215c7a739c7ec96999e8679e7517bae9ca345e9e0`.

The opposite clip explicitly uses a temporal reversal of this simple closed
mobility loop. It is not a second source-captured performance. Normal cadence is
retained; no impacts, gait, flight, holds, equipment action or choreography are
reversed. No frames are synthesized, blended or padded. Final seam quality must
be reviewed in the packaged workout. That review passed for the final 3.75-second
MP4 loops at playback rate 1, including the preparation, movement and rest phases.

The depicted footwear is open-toe strapped footwear. The action needs no toe
separation, special sole or footwear-dependent resistance and is compatible with
ordinary shoes. It uses only the feet for ground support, no equipment and no
travel. Gentle sway stays within the base of support, without impact, forced
foot pivot or lateral drive: Hard Floor and quiet execution are compatible.
The drill does not provide Insect's visible continuous whole-body movement.
Ordinary form checking supplies no audited greatly-benefited mirror criterion.

The anatomy review assigns the anterior/lateral lower-leg and dorsal-foot group
as the direct ankle-control target, with meaningful calf/plantar-foot control as
secondary. The ankles deliberately drive the circular path; these claims are
not incidental balance during an unrelated task. No trunk, hip or shoulder
stabilization is counted. Demand 0 reflects the principal mobility/control task,
not local force or fatigue. Existing exercise claims and ratings are unchanged.
