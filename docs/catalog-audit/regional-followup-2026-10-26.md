# Regional assignment follow-up, catalog 96

The follow-up checked the catalog's contact/equipment restrictions, identical
demonstrations with contradictory physical metadata, the anatomical eligibility
and atomic-sequence filters, and earlier reasons for removing training claims.
It is a bounded audit of clear omissions, not a new approval of every catalog
movement. Duration groupings and exercise identities are unchanged.

Two narrow interpretation errors warranted additive corrections:

- **419, Track Thumb While Nodding the Opposite Way:** restore
  `AnteriorLateralNeckAndHyoidMuscles` and
  `PosteriorNeckAndSuboccipitalMuscles`. Its earlier review removed the neck
  associations because the movement was not resisted neck strengthening.
  Deliberate repeated cervical movement also supplies mobility/motor-control
  work, as already recognized for 491. The packaged loop and the
  [original Physitrack instructions](https://au.physitrack.com/home-exercise-video/x2-gaze-stabilisation-%2528vertical%2529)
  both show intentional opposing head and thumb movement. This is a mobility
  association, not a new strength or flexibility-level assessment.
- **428, 434, 439, 441, 442, 444, 445, 458, 462, 464, 465, 1051:** add
  `AnteriorLateralLowerLegAndDorsalFoot` for reviewed repeated ankle propulsion
  in pogo jumps, line/rotational/forward hops and rope-free jumping. The full
  lateral compartment includes fibularis longus and brevis, which plantarflex
  as well as evert. The older dorsiflexor/foot-clearance argument did not cover
  those muscles. All twelve records visibly repeat loaded ankle propulsion;
  445 remains a component of 465's complete sequence, not an extra selectable
  identity.

The anatomical interpretation is supported by the
[Texas Tech leg anatomy table](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/musculoskeletal_system/leg_tables.html).
[Sunderland Integrated Musculoskeletal Service's peroneal rehabilitation guide](https://www.sunderlandims.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/What-is-peroneal-tendinopathy1.pdf)
includes progressive calf raises and forward/sideways hopping. That supports
the direct loading category; it is not a measurement of muscle recruitment in
each catalog clip. The per-record decision also uses the actual demonstration.
[NHS guidance](https://www.nhsinform.scot/illnesses-and-conditions/muscle-bone-and-joints/neck-and-back-problems-and-conditions/exercises-for-neck-problems)
recognizes repeated head movement as neck exercise without requiring external
resistance.

All primary targets, muscular-demand and fitness ratings, media, side/direction
sequences, timing, identity families and physical modifiers remain unchanged.
Hard Floor and Silence still exclude all twelve hopping records. Ordinary
walking/balance, hip-driven toe taps and posterior calf stretches receive no
new regional credit. Eye-only tracking with the head still receives no neck
credit. Clothing-required standing wall exercises retain their actual torso
or upper-back contact; 913's deeper sustained wall sit is not reclassified from
the shallower 137 demonstration merely because their names are similar.

The shared membership fixture tests both neck groups and all affected lower-leg
resolutions with negative controls. Android and web additionally test actual
planner placement and preservation of unfinished revision-94/95 sessions,
Keeps, phase/slot downvotes, history and exercise scores.

The 13 changed catalog records add 14 secondary associations. The lower-leg
region grows from 40 to 51 schedulable movement identities (12 records, with
445 counted only through 465). The other record restores both neck-region
associations. No new exercises or renamed duplicates were created.

The [SHA-bound review manifest](regional-followup-review-2026-10-26.json) records
exact metadata/media and complete actual-workout playback for all 13 records.

Validation: 1,194 Android tests and 888 web tests passed. The production web
build, parity lock, exact secondary-claim audit and Debug APK packaging passed.
All 13 changed demonstrations were replayed through their complete selection
in the actual web player; 428 was also replayed from the final production build.
