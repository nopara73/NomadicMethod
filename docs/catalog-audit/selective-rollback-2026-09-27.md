## Changes

- Remove yesterday's adaptive partition planner entirely. New workouts keep
  the duration's anatomical partition, even when some exercises have negative
  feedback. Existing sessions keep their recorded custom partitions.
- Match an exercise to a group through its reviewed primary or meaningful
  secondary training. Remove the half-of-leaves threshold and its later
  regional-compound exceptions. Existing ranking, Light, recovery and physical
  restrictions continue to select among eligible movements.
- Restore five distinct selectable movements per required broad modifier
  state, and retain one per fine group. Availability now checks whether the
  complete atomic sequence can fit alongside the other required groups.
  A left/right pair cannot be counted as a one-block alternative.
- Preserve the compatible current atomic exercise after part of it has been
  performed when equipment changes. Previously, shortening a paused workout
  and enabling Wall could remove its second set; the safety check then threw
  instead of applying the change. Native seed 10 and an equivalent web workflow
  reproduce this and now retain the checkpoint and completed work.

There are no catalog, media, anatomy, exercise-ID, score, Keep, recovery-window,
Light-cadence, package-identity or storage-version changes. All 545 records,
including the additions, remain. The catalog revision is still 79.
