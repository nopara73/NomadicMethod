# Shy mode audit

Shy mode is a default-off filter for people who want a less conspicuous
workout in a shared space. The same standard applies whether nearby people are
exercising or not. When enabled, Nomadic Method admits only exercises with an explicit
`Compatible` review; `Incompatible` exercises are removed and unreviewed
metadata fails closed.

The review is based on what the final packaged demonstration visibly asks the
user to do, not the movement's historical practice label. Ordinary,
recognizable strength, cardio, mobility, stretching, balance, arm work, and
familiar boxing mechanics remain available. Actual Tai Chi, Shibashi, Qigong,
dance, ballet presentation, kata-like combat choreography, physique or facial
performance, conspicuous gaze/jaw/head drills, vocal breathing, body tapping,
pelvic or body waves, unusual self-resistance or hand gestures, conspicuous
turning jumps, clapping drills and coordinated punch/hop or arm-circle/cardio
combinations are
excluded. A conventional squat, arm circle, or punch is therefore not removed
merely because its source used a dance, Qigong, or martial-arts label.

The current 569-exercise catalog has 417 compatible and 152 incompatible
records. The October review corrects 29 existing records and excludes six
conspicuous new locomotion drills from Shy. Each incompatible record has one primary visible reason:

| Review reason | Records |
| --- | ---: |
| Physique or facial performance | 13 |
| Gaze, jaw, or conspicuous head drill | 25 |
| Vocal breathing or body tapping | 9 |
| Dance, Tai Chi, Qigong, or flow choreography | 38 |
| Pelvic or body wave | 3 |
| Conspicuous combat or fight choreography | 27 |
| Unusual self-resistance or hand gesture | 2 |
| Conspicuous conditioning or hand choreography | 35 |

The exact exhaustive partition is
[`tools/ExerciseShyCompatibility.psd1`](../tools/ExerciseShyCompatibility.psd1).
Catalog generation rejects missing IDs, duplicates, overlap, unknown IDs, and
mixed Shy classifications inside one atomic exercise sequence. New exercises
therefore remain unreviewed until their complete packaged loop is assessed.

The September boundary correction exposed coverage deficits in restrictive
single/pair profiles. Nomadic Method closed them with three established movements and
exact reviewed human demonstrations: `Fixed-Gaze Head-Turn March`,
`Jab-Cross-Hook-Uppercut Combo`, and
`Jab-Cross-Speed-Bag Combo`. No existing movement was falsely
reclassified, assigned a weaker demand, or given an inflated anatomy or mirror
relationship to satisfy a quota. `Standing Speed-Bag Punches` separately moved
to `BenefitsGreatly` only after its continuous high-elbow circular path was
reviewed against the narrow live-plane/symmetry mirror criterion.

Shy participates in quadratic single/pair availability and materiality checks.
Demand inventories are diagnostics. Existing anatomy exceptions and the separately
accepted Silence/Hard-Floor materiality gap remain unchanged in scope.

Revision 80 preserves exercise identities, scores and anatomically valid Keeps.
Unfinished revision 73–79 workouts retain their original hand sequencing and Shy
semantics until they finish or are explicitly ended; no old movement timer is
transferred to a replacement. New workouts use the corrected catalog. The frozen
revision persists through repeated reopenings on Android and web.

Goalpost Jumping Jacks remain compatible on their own. Their former mandatory
series with Scarecrow Jacks and Arm-Circle Jumping Jacks is separated so Shy can
exclude those conspicuous movements without misclassifying the familiar drill.
Unfinished workouts retain the original series.

The October review also corrects `Alternating Palm-Up Shoulder Rotations` (683)
to Insect-compatible after inspecting its continuous torso and hip turns and
weight shifts. That actual whole-body movement retains genuine primary
rotator-cuff work under Insect plus Shy. Its anatomy and demand are unchanged;
ordinary jumping jacks receive no incidental rotator-cuff training claim.
