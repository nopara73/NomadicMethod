# Short-workout slot variety

## Product contract

New workouts offer **3, 5, 7, 10 and 15 minutes** on Android, the web app and
instant startup controls. Each available slot requires **five distinct approved
selectable session movements** across the existing quadratic modifier-pair
states and compatible Hard Floor checks.

An unfinished longer workout keeps its original duration, complete selections,
progress, history and frozen automatic-Light decision. Its settings allow
shortening, with the current whole atomic selection protected, but cannot
extend it beyond its existing duration. The legacy 20/30-minute resolutions
retain their previous one-choice invariant for restoration; there is no new
three-choice catalog quota or new-workout option above 15 minutes.

A grouped slot accepts genuine primary or meaningful secondary training of
**any** member muscle. It does not require every member muscle in one exercise.
Posture, balance and incidental bracing do not supply training credit. Count
`SessionMovementId` once, and require the complete eligible atomic sequence to
fit: aliases, directions, sides, sets and repetition/hold variants do not
multiply choices. Broad-round training rules, equipment gating, truthful
anatomy and modifier materiality remain in force.

The owner approved three precise additional omissions on 19 October 2026:

| --- | --- |

| `r15.anterior-lateral-lower-leg-dorsal-foot` | Insect |

The normal allocator fills the chosen duration with complete whole-body rounds.
These omissions apply to new plans, not to reopening pre-revision-82 sessions.
The earlier September exceptions and structural pelvic-floor/hand exceptions
remain exact and unchanged. No additional exception was added for the
five-minute head/neck/shoulder-girdle or fifteen-minute arm slot.
