# Hard Floor locomotion review — 31 October 2026

The complete review covers all 44 excluded Room locomotion movements at catalog revision 100. Eleven classifications were overly broad; 33 exclusions remain supported by the packaged action. Room locomotion availability with Floor and Hard Floor enabled rises from seven to 18 distinct movements before fitness, recovery, preferences and the other independent filters are applied. The catalog still contains 51 Room and 10 Field movements.

Hard Floor keeps its existing meaning: an ordinary rigid indoor surface with reduced traction, not a wet or oily floor. Ordinary shoes remain supported. A movement is not excluded merely for using the forefoot, heel, hands, a travel lane or a mat in its demonstration. The review checks the actual loaded contacts, cadence, take-off and landing, sliding, planted pivots and shear. Controlled placements of an unloaded limb are distinguished from forceful push-offs. Difficulty alone is not a surface mechanism.

The promoted floor movements use slow hand-and-foot load transfers comparable to admitted controlled push-ups and reverse tabletop work. No knees, elbows, head, torso or shoulder contacts were erased. The sideways bear-crawl clip still includes knee contact and remains excluded. Frog Jumps remains excluded because the actual demo jumps; this audit does not invent a separate frog-walk variation. Long split-stance lunges, rapid lateral work, acrobatic landings, low alligator crawls and moving handstands retain their exclusions.

Seated Buttock Walks remains excluded, but its reason is corrected from forbidden contact to concentrated repeated hip loading. Hips are not a globally forbidden contact. Gorilla Crawl remains excluded and is assigned to repeated hand-supported foot landings.

These are observational product judgments, not measured friction certifications. [HSE guidance](https://www.hse.gov.uk/slips/footprocure.htm) establishes that grip depends on the actual footwear and surface; it does not certify these exercises. [NHS inform](https://www.nhsinform.scot/healthy-living/preventing-falls/keeping-well/strength-and-balance-exercises) describes controlled toe and heel walking; this supports identifying the movement rather than a claim about every surface.

The [machine-readable review](catalog-audit/hard-floor-locomotion-review-2026-10-31.json) pins all 44 names, contact metadata and exact packaged MP4 hashes. Android and web contract checks consume the same review. No anatomy, intensity, scores, Keeps, movement identities, footage, selection priorities or locomotion quotas change. The catalog/database revision refreshes metadata in place, and unfinished-workout restoration is checked separately.
